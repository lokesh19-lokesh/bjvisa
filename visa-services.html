Standard Document Checklist

General items required across international visa applications:

  • Original Passport: Minimum 6 months validity & 2 blank pages.
  • Photographs: 35x45mm, 80% face coverage, white background.
  • Financials: 6 months bank statement with seal & 3 years ITR.
  • Employment Proof: Leave approval letter & salary slips.
  • Accommodation & Flights: Confirmed reservations.
