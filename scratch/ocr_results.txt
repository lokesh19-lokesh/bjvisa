WHAT IS VAYD?
VAYD (Visa At Your Doorstep) is a premium biometric
enrolment service available for eligible Schengen and
UK visa applications.
It enables applicants to complete biometric enrolment at their
preferred location-such as their residence, office, or corporate
premises-instead of visiting the VFS Visa Application Centre,
subject to Embassy and VFS Global eligibility and availability.
This premium service offers greater convenience, privacy,
and time savings for eligible applicants.
