Your Trusted Visa Consultant in Hyderabad

Based in Hyderabad, Telangana, BJ VISA CONSULTANCY provides professional visa consultancy, documentation guidance and application-related assistance for individuals, families, business travellers and travel industry partners.

Professional Guidance

Making Requirements Easier to Understand

We understand that international visa requirements can sometimes feel complicated. Different destinations have different rules, supporting document requirements and application procedures. Even knowing where to begin can be overwhelming, especially for first-time travellers.

Our role is to make those requirements easier to understand.

We help clients understand the applicable process, assess their documentation requirements, prepare supporting documents and navigate relevant appointment and submission procedures.

OUR PURPOSE

Clear, Confident & Reassuring

Our services include assistance for Schengen visas, UK visas, tourist visas, business visas, family visit visas and selected other international destinations.

We also provide premium Visa At Your Doorstep (VAYD) assistance for eligible UK and Schengen applicants, subject to applicable service conditions and availability.

"Our purpose is simple: to help you approach your international travel plans with greater clarity, confidence and peace of mind."

WHY WE DO WHAT WE DO

Because Your Journey Is Personal.

Behind every enquiry is a person with plans, expectations and something important at stake.

Family Holiday Dreams

Imagine planning a holiday with your family for months, only to feel overwhelmed by unfamiliar documentation requirements.

Crucial Business Ventures

Imagine preparing for an important business trip while trying to understand a visa process you have never experienced before.

Long-Awaited Reunions

We cannot remove every uncertainty from the visa process. Requirements can vary, and final decisions belong to the relevant authorities.

OUR APPROACH

People First, Paperwork Second

Five core commitments guiding how we handle your application.

We Understand Before We Advise

No two applicants are exactly alike. Your travel purpose, personal circumstances and supporting documents all matter. We take these factors into account when guiding you through the applicable process.

We Believe in Honest Answers

We explain what our services can and cannot do. If something needs attention in your documentation, we believe you deserve to know about it.

We Treat Documentation Seriously

Accurate and consistent documentation is an important part of preparing a visa application. We help identify missing information and guide you through the relevant requirements.

We Respect Your Privacy

Visa applications may involve passports, financial statements, employment records and other personal information. We understand the importance of handling these documents responsibly and confidentially.

We Stay Committed to Ethical Processing

We do not encourage fabricated documents, false information, misrepresentation or shortcuts that violate official requirements. We believe trust is built by doing things responsibly, even when the responsible answer takes more explanation.
