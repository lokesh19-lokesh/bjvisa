2. Travel Insurance

Medical emergencies abroad can lead to crippling expenses. We issue government and embassy-approved travel insurance covering medical hospitalization, emergency evacuation, baggage loss, passport theft, and unexpected trip cancellations.

  • Mandatory Schengen policy compliant with minimum €30,000 medical coverage.
  • Zero-deductible claims assistance and 24/7 global emergency helpline.
  • Coverage for pre-existing emergency stabilization and COVID-19 care.

3. Air Ticketing Assistance

Most embassies recommend submitting flight reservations rather than purchasing non-refundable air tickets before visa issuance. We generate authentic, verifiable round-trip flight itineraries for your application dossier, and help you lock in competitive airfares once the visa is approved.

  • Verifiable flight itineraries for embassy submission without upfront full purchase risks.
  • Competitive international airfare pricing across premium full-service airlines.
  • Special assistance for baggage allowances, layovers, and transit visas.

4. Hotel Reservation Assistance

Consulates demand authentic proof of accommodation that strictly correlates with your travel itinerary dates and locations. We provide confirmed hotel bookings across trusted global hospitality chains with flexible cancellation terms.

  • Legitimate hotel booking vouchers carrying complete hotel contact details.
  • Strategic city-center locations convenient for tourism and public transit.
  • Flexibility to modify dates or upgrade once your travel schedule is confirmed.

5. Travel Planning Guidance

Navigating immigration protocols, forex card options, overseas SIM cards, customs regulations, and local transit passes can be overwhelming. We equip our travelers with practical, up-to-date travel dossiers before departure.

  • Pre-departure briefing covering immigration counter interview tips.
  • Guidance on multi-currency travel forex cards & international e-SIMs.
  • Destination safety, local laws, transit cards, and seasonal packing tips.
