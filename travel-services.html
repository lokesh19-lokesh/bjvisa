OUR SPECIALIZED SERVICES

What We Offer

From passport issuance and flight bookings to private transfers and customized tour packages, discover our specialized travel support.

Passport Services Travel Insurance Flight Tickets Hotel Booking Transportation Tour Packages

1. Passport Services

A valid passport is the essential first step of every international journey. We provide end-to-end guidance and appointment coordination for Indian passport applications, renewals, Tatkaal express processing, ECR/Non-ECR status modification, minor/spouse endorsements, and police clearance certificate (PCC) assistance at Passport Seva Kendras (PSK/POPSK).

  • Fresh passport applications, renewals & Tatkaal urgent appointment booking.
  • Comprehensive document scrutiny to avoid query slips or PSK resubmissions.
  • Assistance with damaged/lost passports, name corrections, and address endorsements.

2. Travel Insurance Assistance

Medical emergencies abroad can lead to crippling expenses. We issue government and embassy-approved travel insurance covering medical hospitalization, emergency evacuation, baggage loss, passport theft, and unexpected trip cancellations.

  • Mandatory Schengen policy compliant with minimum €30,000 medical coverage.
  • Zero-deductible claims assistance and 24/7 global emergency helpline.
  • Coverage for trip delays, lost baggage, medical evacuation, and COVID-19 care.

3. Flight Ticket Assistance

Most embassies recommend submitting verifiable flight reservations rather than purchasing non-refundable air tickets before visa issuance. We generate authentic, verifiable round-trip flight itineraries for your application dossier, and help you lock in competitive airfares once the visa is approved.

  • Verifiable flight itineraries for embassy submission without upfront full purchase risks.
  • Competitive international airfare pricing across premium full-service airlines.
  • Special assistance for baggage allowances, layovers, and transit visas.

4. Hotel Booking Assistance

Consulates demand authentic proof of accommodation that strictly correlates with your travel itinerary dates and locations. We provide confirmed hotel bookings across trusted global hospitality chains with flexible cancellation terms.

  • Legitimate hotel booking vouchers carrying complete hotel contact details.
  • Strategic city-center locations convenient for tourism and public transit.
  • Flexibility to modify dates or upgrade once your travel schedule is confirmed.

5. Transportation Arrangements

Arrive at your international destination with total confidence and ease. We arrange dedicated airport meet-and-greet transfers, private chauffeur services, intercity transfers, and international rail passes (such as Eurail, Swiss Travel Pass, and Japan Rail Pass) tailored to your exact travel itinerary.

  • Pre-arranged private airport arrivals and departures with professional drivers.
  • International train reservations, Eurail passes & intercity express coaches.
  • Chauffeur-driven executive cars, family minivans, and local rental coordination.

6. International Tour Packages

We curate bespoke holiday and tour packages designed for families, honeymooners, solo explorers, and group travelers. Whether discovering European historical landmarks, tropical Maldives escapes, or cultural immersion in Japan, our packages include hand-picked itineraries and complete visa guidance.

  • Customized daily itineraries matched with visa requirements.
  • Curated luxury, premium, and budget-friendly stay options.
  • Guided sightseeing tours, museum skip-the-line passes & transfers.
