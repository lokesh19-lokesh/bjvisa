ETHICS & GOVERNANCE

Compliance & Business Policy

At BJ VISA CONSULTANCY, we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of professional ethics, regulatory compliance, and responsible visa processing.

Our Operational Policy

  • Authentic & Verifiable Documentation: We process visa applications only with genuine, authentic, and verifiable documents provided by applicants or partner agencies.
  • Zero Tolerance for Fabricated Documents: We do not prepare, alter, fabricate, manipulate, or facilitate false, forged, or misleading documents under any circumstances.
  • Regulatory Guidelines Compliance: All applications are processed strictly in accordance with respective Embassy, Consulate, and VFS Global guidelines.
  • Proactive Remediation: If any document is incomplete or does not meet required standards, we guide our travel partners in obtaining appropriate genuine supporting records before submission.

Our Partner Assurance

  • Your Clients Always Remain Yours: We operate exclusively as your backend processing partner. We never contact, solicit, market to, or attempt to acquire your clients directly.
  • 100% Client Data Confidentiality: Strict data protection protocols ensure your clients' identification, financial papers, and passports are treated with maximum security.
  • Long-Term Mutual Growth: We believe in building enduring business relationships based on trust, integrity, professionalism, and mutual prosperity.
  • No Unrealistic Guarantees: We maintain complete honesty. Visa decisions rest solely with embassies, and we ensure compliance to give applications their strongest legitimate merit.
