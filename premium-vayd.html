WHAT IS VAYD?

The Ultimate in Visa Convenience

VAYD (Visa At Your Doorstep) is a specialized biometric enrolment service available for eligible Schengen and UK visa applications.

Rather than visiting the crowded VFS Visa Application Centre, eligible applicants can complete their mandatory biometric enrolment (digital fingerprint scanning and facial photographs) at their preferred location—such as their private residence, executive office suite, or corporate headquarters.

BJ Visa Consultancy coordinates the entire dossier preparation, scheduling, and protocol management with certified VFS biometric teams to ensure a smooth, private, and discreet experience.

Eligibility & Availability Notice:

Doorstep biometric enrolment is subject to applicable Embassy and VFS Global guidelines, location feasibility, and slot availability.

Certified & Authorized

Processed strictly via authorized consular mobile biometric systems.

EAMLESS WORKFLOW

How The VAYD Process Works

From preliminary file review to doorstep mobile biometrics, our team handles every detail.

01
Initial Consultation

We assess your travel dates, preferred destination, and confirm eligibility for Visa At Your Doorstep services.

02
Document Scrutiny

Our specialists verify all financial, occupational, and itinerary records against consular standards before filing.

03
Doorstep Slot Booking

We formally coordinate with the authorized mobile biometric team for a preferred date and time at your location.

04
Doorstep Appointment

Authorized personnel arrive at your chosen address equipped with mobile biometric kits to record fingerprints & photos.

05
Consular Processing

Your sealed documents and biometric tokens are securely transmitted to the relevant Embassy / Consulate for evaluation.

06
Secure Passport Return

Once the decision is rendered, your passport is returned securely via insured courier directly to your doorstep.
